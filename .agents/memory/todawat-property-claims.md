---
name: Todawat property claims
description: Guardrails for property details published on the Todawat Homes & Builders website.
---

Treat approval status, prices, and availability as specific to each listing. Do not imply that every property is JDA/RERA approved or currently available without a current source for that property.

**Why:** The supplied Instagram snapshots do not establish approval status, price, or availability for every property.

**How to apply:** When adding or revising property copy, publish those claims only when the business confirms them for the individual listing.
